focus

Study first, then spend what you earn.

KotlinAGPL-3.0★ 6betaAndroid
View the source Documentation

Focus Study Gate turns a study plan into a reward system and puts a soft gate in front of the apps that eat your time. You split the day into subjects with task lists and generous timers; finishing tasks fills a daily treat bank of minutes, and those minutes are what you spend at the gate.

Everything runs on the phone. There is no account, no server, and the manifest declares no internet permission.

What it does

  • Subjects and tasks

    Per-subject checklists with a pausable, extendable timer, an alarm with sound, and spoken reminders when you run over. Long-press a card to edit, delete or change its media.

  • Treat bank

    Each subject's reward is split across its tasks and paid out as you tick them off. The bank resets daily.

  • Weekly tasks

    Do them any day of the week. The sooner you finish, the bigger the multiplier — x2 down to x1, then x0.5 once late.

  • Soft gate

    Pick which tracked apps get gated. Opening one shows a "what for?" overlay with a short countdown and a choice of passes.

  • Not-free blocks

    Scheduled windows where only an allow-list of apps works. Optional strict mode removes the emergency override.

  • Shorts and Reels counting

    Detects YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, counts swipes, and lets you leave.

  • Strict Shorts mode

    The gate opens at once with no "finish this one", and the only way on is a sentence you type by hand (paste is blocked).

  • Usage screen

    Digital Wellbeing-style screen time for every app: week chart, donut, per-app list, hourly strip and day-by-day history.

  • Notes

    Text, checklists, rich formatting, labels, pin, archive, trash, search, voice notes and attachments (photos, videos, files, links, linked notes).

  • PDF library and reader

    Built in, with hand-off to Episteme Reader if you have it installed.

  • Motivation

    Streaks, XP and levels, 46 badges (15 hidden "Honest Moments" ones), 17 short games for breaks, and four home-screen widgets.

  • Parked thoughts

    Capture a distracting idea straight into Notes without leaving the subject.

Install

  1. 1

    Build from source

    git clone https://github.com/CodeVortax/focus
cd focus
# local.properties must contain sdk.dir=/path/to/android-sdk
gradle assembleDebug
# APK: app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
  2. 2

    Install on the phone

    adb install -r app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
  3. 3

    First run

    The home screen guides you through three grants: Usage access, Display over other apps and, for Shorts counting, the Accessibility service. Then set Battery → Unrestricted so your ROM does not kill the watcher.

How to use it

Daily loop

Set up subjects and tasks, run a subject timer, tick tasks off to fill the treat bank, then spend minutes at the gate on Shorts, gated apps or the built-in player. When the bank is empty the gate offers short free passes behind a countdown, or lets you leave.

Good to know

  • Currently a beta. Expect rough edges and back up your data before updating.
  • No internet permission: usage, notes, settings and rewards never leave the device.
  • Leave presses Back to exit most apps, but sends browsers Home so your tabs and history are kept.
  • Focus reuses the swipe-counting method from swipe-tally for its Shorts counter.
