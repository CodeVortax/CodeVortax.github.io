crystal-alarm

A clear, private alarm clock for Android.

KotlinAGPL-3.0★ 1appAndroid
View the source Documentation

A glass-themed alarm clock with a hand-drawn analog face, countdown timer, stopwatch and Material You theming. Everything is stored locally on the phone, and the app requests no internet access at all.

What it does

  • Set and forget

    Create one-shot or repeating alarms, with any combination of days.

  • Sound picker

    Choose from system alarm, notification or ringtone sounds.

  • Volume ramp

    Optional: starts quiet and grows louder over 15 seconds so you wake gently.

  • Puzzle dismiss

    Solve a simple math problem before the alarm stops, so you cannot turn it off half-asleep.

  • Fullscreen alert

    The alarm opens a bright full-screen activity even when the phone is locked.

  • Timer

    Pomodoro, short break and long break presets plus a custom timer, a circular countdown ring, and pause / resume / add a minute on the fly.

  • Stopwatch

    Lap timing with millisecond precision at 20 fps, laps listed newest first.

  • The clock face

    Radial gradient background, a specular highlight sweeping slowly around the face, a smooth second hand with a counterweight tail, and a digital overlay.

  • Settings shortcuts

    One tap to disable battery optimisation, adjust notification settings, or grant exact alarm permission on Android 14+.

  2. 2

    Requirements

    Android SDK 34 and JDK 17. The repo's building guide covers the full setup.

How to use it

Everyday use

Add alarms from the clock screen, pick days, a sound and whether you want the volume ramp or the puzzle. The timer and stopwatch live in the same app, and the theme follows your wallpaper colours on Android 12+.

Good to know

  • Smart snooze (flip the phone over to snooze) is planned but not implemented yet.
  • Some settings screens deep-link straight into Android's own battery and notification pages, because only the system can change those.
