About

Small tools that do one thing well, and keep your data on your own device.

Android apps

Kotlin apps built around privacy: no accounts, no analytics, no internet permission where it isn't needed. focus, Crystal and swipe-tally all run fully offline.

ESP32 firmware

Boards that serve their own web app over WiFi — an IR test bench, and a repeater that meters and limits each device — so the phone in your hand is the control panel.

Termux tooling

Most of the Python here is made to work from a phone: bridging USB to esptool, reading right-to-left text, QEMU, and downloads that survive a dropped connection.
